App Walkthrough

  1. Pick a driver from the dropdown.
  2. Tap Accept Job then Start Navigation — departure time is recorded.
  3. Tap Mark Arrived — an arrival confirmation is sent to admin.
  4. Tap Complete Delivery — delivered time, destination, and Google Maps pin sync to admin.
  5. Use Check In and Log Fuel for the other driver actions.
